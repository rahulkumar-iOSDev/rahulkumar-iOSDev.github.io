Case studies

How I approached three of my projects.

Tata CLiQ: stability and speed

Keeping a large e-commerce app stable and quick while the team keeps shipping features.

PlatformiPhone and iPad
StackSwift, UIKit, SwiftUI
ArchitectureMVVM/MVC, async/await

Problem

The crash-free user rate was 97%, the Product Detail and Loyalty pages needed faster load times, and the app download size was 141 MB.

Approach

I ran root-cause crash analysis (monitored in Crashlytics), profiled and optimized memory, and refactored legacy UIKit screens to SwiftUI where it made sense.

Quality

I wrote XCTest unit tests for business logic and ViewModels, took part in code reviews, and used CI/CD pipelines for automated build, test and release.

Impact

The crash-free rate rose to 99.75%, key pages loaded 30–40% faster, and the app shrank to 124 MB (about 12% smaller).
